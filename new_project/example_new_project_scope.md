# Example New Project Scope Requiring an Estimate

Approximately 18,000 square foot retail build-out in an existing leased commercial space in a suburban retail plaza. The space was previously occupied by a different retailer and will require selective interior demolition before new work can begin.

Anticipated scope includes:

- Interior demolition of prior tenant improvements
- New metal-stud partitions for sales floor, fitting rooms, staff areas, and back-of-house storage
- New flooring, paint, doors, hardware, and ceiling grid
- Lighting replacement and lighting controls
- HVAC modifications, balancing, and controls coordination
- Electrical panel upgrades and distribution for sales floor, back-of-house, signage, security, and point-of-sale locations
- Washroom upgrades, including fixtures, finishes, and accessibility review
- Back-of-house storage shelving support and owner fixture coordination
- Point-of-sale rough-ins and low-voltage pathways
- Exterior and interior signage coordination with landlord criteria
- Commissioning, closeout documentation, and owner turnover support

Assumptions and constraints:

- Existing rooftop HVAC units are assumed to be reusable with modifications.
- Existing electrical service capacity has not yet been confirmed.
- Owner-provided loose fixtures, merchandise displays, security equipment, and IT equipment are excluded, but supports and rough-ins are included.
- Work is expected to occur in an unoccupied tenant space, with coordination required for adjacent plaza tenants.
- Landlord design review, municipal permitting, utility review, and owner finish approvals are required before construction.
