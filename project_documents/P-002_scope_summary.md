# P-002 Scope Summary

This project was a retail renovation inside an enclosed shopping mall. The scope included selective interior demolition, storefront finish repairs, ceiling and lighting upgrades, fitting room modifications, flooring replacement, display fixture relocation, POS rough-ins, limited washroom refresh work, mall-compliant signage, and phased closeout.

The estimate assumed access through the mall loading dock, use of a freight elevator, and a mix of normal-hours and after-hours work. Owner-provided merchandising fixtures were excluded, but fixture relocation, temporary protection, and coordination were included. Base-building systems were expected to remain operational, with limited HVAC tie-ins and electrical shutdowns coordinated through mall operations.

The project was delivered under a construction manager at risk model. Work occurred in a partially occupied environment, with portions of the tenant space and adjacent mall areas remaining active. Delivery constraints were significant: loading windows were limited, noisy work was restricted, material staging was tight, and some work had to be sequenced around mall events. The final scope required more phasing and supervision than the original estimate reflected.
