# P-004 Scope Summary

This project was a medical clinic tenant improvement in an existing commercial building. The project included selective demolition, new exam rooms, treatment rooms, reception and waiting areas, staff areas, accessible washrooms, clinical casework, cleanable wall protection, plumbing rough-ins, HVAC zoning, specialty exhaust coordination, electrical upgrades, low-voltage pathways, and commissioning.

The scope excluded owner-provided medical equipment, loose furniture, and clinical technology devices, but included supports, rough-ins, and coordination for those systems. The estimate assumed that specialty systems could be coordinated through standard design review and that accessibility requirements were mostly addressed in the initial drawings.

The site was partially occupied by adjacent tenants, and work required careful coordination for noise, dust, deliveries, and shutdowns. The project used a design-build delivery model with significant owner and consultant involvement. Although the interior construction complexity is similar to a retail build-out, the use case is different. Medical room layouts, plumbing density, inspection expectations, and specialty system coordination make this a useful but imperfect comparator.
