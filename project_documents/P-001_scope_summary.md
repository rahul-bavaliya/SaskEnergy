# P-001 Scope Summary

This project was a suburban retail tenant build-out in a leased plaza space. The work included interior demolition of prior tenant improvements, new metal-stud partitions, sales floor finishes, back-of-house storage rooms, ceiling grid replacement, lighting upgrades, HVAC modifications, washroom upgrades, point-of-sale rough-ins, signage coordination, and commissioning.

The project assumed that the existing landlord-provided electrical service had enough spare capacity for the new tenant load with only panel-level upgrades. It also assumed that rooftop HVAC units were serviceable and could be modified rather than replaced. Owner-provided retail fixtures and selected equipment were excluded from the contractor's base scope, although blocking, power, and coordination for those items were included.

Delivery was lump sum with a compressed pre-opening schedule. The site was not occupied during construction, but the team needed to coordinate with adjacent plaza tenants for deliveries, noise, and shutdowns. Municipal permitting was straightforward, while landlord design review required several coordination meetings for signage, storefront finishes, and utility access. Commissioning was included for lighting controls, HVAC balancing, and electrical closeout documentation.
