# P-006 Scope Summary

This project was a small cosmetic retail refresh in an occupied street-front retail space. The scope included selective removal of existing finishes and fixtures, new paint, replacement flooring, ceiling tile touch-ups, a lighting refresh, minor power relocations, display fixture updates, cash-wrap modifications, interior branding, and exterior sign panel replacement.

The project excluded major HVAC work, electrical service upgrades, washroom renovations, structural changes, and full commissioning. Owner-provided loose fixtures and merchandise displays were mostly excluded, although installation support and coordination were included for selected items. The estimate assumed that existing systems were adequate and that work could be completed during a short closure and limited after-hours windows.

Delivery was lump sum with a two-month schedule. Because the site was occupied before and after the short closure, the team had to coordinate material deliveries, temporary customer access restrictions, and owner merchandising decisions. The project uses retail terminology similar to larger build-outs, but its scope was primarily cosmetic and much smaller than a mechanical/electrical-heavy tenant build-out.
