# P-003 Scope Summary

This project was a ground-up small retail shell completed in 2018. The work included civil site preparation, grading, utility extensions, stormwater infrastructure, foundations, steel framing, roof deck, exterior envelope, base-building HVAC equipment, electrical service, site lighting, plumbing rough-ins, limited shell finishes, and signage infrastructure.

The project excluded tenant-specific sales floor finishes, retail fixtures, POS systems, tenant branding, and detailed interior build-out work. The estimate assumed average soil conditions, standard utility connection requirements, and a conventional schedule without major weather impacts. Because the project was new construction, the main drivers were sitework, structure, envelope, and utility connections rather than interior demolition or tenant coordination.

Delivery was design-bid-build. The site was unoccupied, which simplified staging and work hours compared with retail renovations. However, the project required more front-end civil coordination, entitlement review, and utility company involvement than a tenant build-out would require. While it is related to retail construction, it is not a strong direct comparator for an 18,000 square foot retail tenant build-out in an existing plaza space.
