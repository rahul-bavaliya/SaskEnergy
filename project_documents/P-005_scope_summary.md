# P-005 Scope Summary

This project involved converting part of a warehouse and light industrial building into office and support space. The scope included selective demolition, slab trenching, new washrooms, break areas, office partitions, acoustic treatments, ceiling systems, lighting, electrical distribution, data pathways, HVAC zoning, warehouse ventilation adjustments, and commissioning.

The project excluded tenant furniture, IT equipment, racking modifications outside the office conversion area, and final warehouse process equipment. The estimate assumed that record drawings were sufficiently accurate for underground plumbing and that rooftop HVAC equipment would arrive within the planned procurement window.

The site was partially occupied, with warehouse activity continuing outside the construction zone. Work sequencing had to maintain access for operations, deliveries, and fire egress. The project was managed under a construction manager at risk model. It shares some overlap with retail work in finishes, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and washrooms, but the building type and operational drivers are different. Final cost reconciliation is not complete, so the available actual cost data is incomplete and should be treated carefully.
