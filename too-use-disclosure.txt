## APIs and Models

**Nvidia API**
- `nvidia/nv-embed-v1` — embedding model 
- `google/gemma-3n-e2b-it` — LLM used for two purposes:
  1. Structured field extraction from the new project brief (JSON output)
  2. Grounded synthesis of the estimating support report

Both were selected for accessibility and cost-effectiveness during the
assessment. In a production SaskEnergy deployment, these would be replaced
with Azure OpenAI endpoints (Azure OpenAI `text-embedding-ada-002` and
`gpt-4o`) to keep data within the enterprise boundary. The client
initialisation is a one-line change — the rest of the pipeline is identical.

## What Reflects My Own Work and Judgment

- Reading and analysing all 6 historical projects to identify which were
  valid comparators and which were not (P-006, P-003 exclusions)
- Identifying P-005's incomplete actuals as a governance risk and designing
  the specific control around it
- Deciding to implement hybrid retrieval rather than semantic search alone,
  based on understanding the data's vocabulary overlap problem
- Choosing to implement cosine similarity from scratch rather than importing
  a library, to keep the logic fully visible and explainable
- Structuring the system prompt in `synthesizer.py` to enforce grounding,
  citation by project ID, and the mandatory human review statement
- All governance thresholds (similarity floor 0.80, escalation cutoff 2022,
  size mismatch tolerance 35%) were set based on my own reading of the data
- The decision to use LLM-based field extraction over regex for the project
  brief parser, and the reasoning behind it
